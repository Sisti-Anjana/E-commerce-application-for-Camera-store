Professional-Grade Performance

The Canon EOS R5 Mark II represents the pinnacle of mirrorless camera technology. Built for professional photographers and videographers who demand nothing less than perfection, the R5 II combines a high-resolution 45MP sensor with blazing-fast continuous shooting speeds of up to 30 fps with the electronic shutter. The advanced subject-detection autofocus system recognizes people, animals, vehicles, and aircraft, ensuring tack-sharp focus in every situation.

Video capabilities are equally impressive, with internal 8K 60p RAW and 4K 120p recording. The improved heat dissipation system allows extended recording times, making it ideal for documentary and commercial work. Dual card slots (CFexpress and SD UHS-II) provide flexible and reliable storage options for any workflow.
